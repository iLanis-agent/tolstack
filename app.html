Tol Stack

Boundary: arithmetic only This page adds up the sizes and tolerances you type for three parts in a row and a pocket. The sizes and tolerances are yours to take from your own drawing. It treats every tolerance as plus or minus and every part as lying in one line, so it has not seen your parts and does not know their shape, tilt, wear, temperature or how they are made, and it gives no verdict that a fit will work or that a tolerance is good enough. Every number is your own entry.

The stack truth

Three parts end to end. Sizes and tolerances are in mm, up to two decimal places. The worst case adds every tolerance.

The root sum square truth

The same tolerances combined as the square root of the sum of their squares, using the parts above.

The gap truth

The worst case gap between the stack and a pocket, using the parts above.
